## Priority Issues

**[P1] The page is responsible but not distinctive**  
Why it matters: visitors may trust it but not remember it. Without real testimonials yet, the product artifact and brand point of view must carry memorability.  
Fix: make the hero and generator feel like an inspectable analytical instrument: numbered balls, filter matrices, draw-history strips, spreadsheet evidence, or a real product screenshot.  
Suggested command: `$impeccable bolder index.html`

**[P1] The generator is under-promoted as conversion proof**  
Why it matters: PRODUCT.md says the generator is low-friction proof of value, but it sits mid-page and looks like another panel.  
Fix: move a compact generator preview or live sample closer to the first viewport, or redesign the generator section as the visual peak of the page.  
Suggested command: `$impeccable layout index.html`

**[P1] Portuguese copy without accents damages trust**  
Why it matters: a product selling precision cannot look imprecise in its own language. Strings like “Precos”, “criterio”, “analise”, “nao”, “premio”, and “apos” read unfinished for Brazilian users.  
Fix: restore proper pt-BR accents in metadata, nav, headings, labels, FAQ, generator status, and button copy.  
Suggested command: `$impeccable clarify index.html`

**[P2] Equal-weight cards flatten hierarchy**  
Why it matters: proof strip, method, features, VIP routine, generator, pricing, and FAQ use similar visual grammar. The user has to infer what matters most.  
Fix: reduce repeated card grids, vary section composition, and give generator/offer a richer treatment than support content.  
Suggested command: `$impeccable distill index.html`

**[P2] Generator can silently violate selected criteria**  
Why it matters: `generateUniqueGame` falls back to a random game after attempts. For a brand built on “critérios”, silent fallback is a trust problem.  
Fix: return failure when criteria cannot be satisfied and ask users to relax filters instead of displaying a possibly non-compliant game.  
Suggested command: `$impeccable harden app.js`

## Persona Red Flags

**Jordan, first-timer**: understands “sem promessa de prêmio”, but may not understand “soma mínima”, “moldura”, “miolo”, or “repetidas”. The generator needs inline guidance near each criterion.

**Riley, stress tester**: will catch that criteria can fail silently. They may also hit a broken conversion path if no email client is configured for `mailto:` links.

**Casey, distracted mobile user**: loses nav and header CTA on mobile. Returning to pricing or generator after interruption requires scrolling and reorientation.

**Lotofácil analyst persona**: trusts the ethics, but may not yet believe analytical depth. The page names criteria but does not show enough evidence, such as frequency samples, historical comparison, or a filter explanation trail.
